Tips for the best TikTok download quality

Use a full public TikTok link, make sure the video is still online, and keep your browser up to date. If a download looks small or blurry, the creator may have uploaded it in lower resolution. For the smoothest results on mobile, use a stable Wi-Fi or 4G connection.

Why a TikTok download might fail

Downloads usually fail for three reasons: the video is private or was deleted, the link has extra characters or is incomplete, or TikTok is temporarily slow to respond. Copy the link again from the Share menu, wait a moment and retry. Private videos can never be downloaded.
